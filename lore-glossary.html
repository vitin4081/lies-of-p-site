The Krat Glossary

Essential concepts, phenomena, and factions that define the world of Lies of P.

Energy Source

Ergo

A mysterious blue power source discovered deep beneath Krat. It fueled the city's rapid industrial revolution, powering all puppets. However, Ergo is not merely energy—it stores human memories, ego, and souls, which eventually led to puppets awakening self-awareness.

The Rulebook

The Grand Covenant

The four absolute laws programmed into every puppet by Geppetto and Venigni to ensure safety:

  • Rule 1: All puppets must obey their creator's commands.
  • Rule 2: A puppet cannot harm a human.
  • Rule 3: A puppet protects and serves humans and Krat.
  • Rule 4: A puppet cannot lie.
The Plague

Petrification Disease

A deadly, agonizing plague sweeping through Krat. It turns flesh into stone, causes blindness, and eventually mutates victims into horrific Carcass monsters. Its true origin is tied directly to the Alchemists' unethical experiments with Ergo.

Faction

The Alchemists

A powerful secret society led by Simon Manus. Originally researchers who discovered Ergo and cured diseases, they degenerated into religious fanatics seeking to force humanity's "evolution" into divine beings, regardless of the body count.

Faction

The Stalkers

Mercenaries and hunters who wear animal masks to conceal their identities. Divided into factions like the aristocratic Bastards and the working-class Sweepers, they were hired for protection, monster hunting, and dirty work in Krat.

The Catastrophe

Puppet Frenzy

The tragic event where almost all puppets in Krat simultaneously bypassed the Grand Covenant and slaughtered the citizens. The outbreak turned Krat from a utopian paradise into an abandoned, blood-soaked graveyard overnight.
