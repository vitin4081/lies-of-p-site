King's Flame, Fuoco

The Furnace Puppet

A massive, heavily armored factory furnace puppet located deep within Venigni Works. Originally designed to manage the factory's smelting processes, it now incinerates anything that crosses its path, leaving behind a trail of oil and fire.

Combat Tips

Fuoco relies on wide, heavy swings and devastating fire area-of-effect (AoE) attacks. When he glows red and charges forward, either execute a Perfect Guard or run out of range. In Phase 2, he will shoot tar onto the floor and unleash a massive fire wave; immediately hide behind the red pipes/pillars scattered around the arena to survive the blast.

Combat Stats

  • Type: Puppet
  • Weakness: Electric Blitz
  • Resistance: Fire
  • Location: Center of Venigni Works
  • Summon: Specter highly recommended
  • Drop: King's Flame Ergo, High-powered Flame Amplifier, Flame Grindstone
