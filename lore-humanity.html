The Weight of a Lie

"A puppet cannot lie." — The Grand Covenant, Rule 4.

Unlike every other automaton in Krat, P possesses a unique anomaly: the ability to deceive. Throughout your journey, you are forced to choose between the harsh truth and comforting lies. These choices do not merely change dialogue; they accumulate, fundamentally altering P's internal Ergo and his very nature.

Signs of Awakening

System Messages

As you lie, the game signals your internal shift. It begins with "Your springs are reacting," evolves to "The Ergo is whispering," and ultimately peaks at "Your heart is pounding." These represent the mechanical body yielding to a human soul.

The Spring Cat

Animals instinctively fear puppets. Early in the game, Hotel Krat's resident cat will hiss and avoid P. As your humanity level rises, the cat allows you to pet it, and eventually, it will affectionately jump into your arms.

Portrait of a Boy

Hidden in Geppetto's study is a wooden portrait of P. With every significant lie told, the wooden nose on the painting grows longer. Maxing out your humanity allows you to extract a unique weapon from this very portrait.

Warmth & Emotion

Humanity unlocks new interactions. You gain the ability to listen to musical records, which further stimulates P's emotions, and you can eventually interact with the Hotel's piano to play a perfect melody rather than discordant notes.

The Final Choice (Endings)

Ending Requirement Outcome
Real Boy: They All Lived Happily Ever After Give your heart to Geppetto. (Humanity level does not matter). P surrenders his heart. Geppetto uses it to revive Carlo, replacing the survivors of Hotel Krat with perfect, obedient puppet replicas.
Free From the Puppet String Refuse to give your heart, but possess low humanity. P defeats the Nameless Puppet and survives as a free automaton, unchained by his creator but still fundamentally a machine.
Rise of P Refuse to give your heart, possess high humanity, and give Sophia peace. P defeats the Nameless Puppet. He sheds a tear, proving he has become truly human, and transfers his Ergo to revive Sophia.
