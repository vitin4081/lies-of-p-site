The Truth Behind Krat

Once a beacon of technological progress and prosperity thanks to the miraculous power of Ergo, the city of Krat has fallen into ruin. The Puppet Frenzy turned the city's tireless servants into relentless murderers, while the Petrification Disease turns the remaining survivors into monsters.

As a puppet created by Geppetto, you must navigate this nightmare, uncover the dark secrets of the Alchemists, and discover what it truly means to be human. Choose your path to unravel the mysteries of this fallen utopia.
