The Fall of Krat

A chronological record of the city's rise to glory and descent into madness.

The Discovery of Ergo

Alchemists unearth a miraculous blue mineral beneath Krat. This limitless energy source cures diseases and sparks an unprecedented industrial revolution, transforming Krat into a beacon of global progress.

The Golden Age of Puppets

Inventors Geppetto and Lorenzini Venigni create autonomous puppets powered by Ergo. To ensure public safety, The Grand Covenant is established, binding all puppets to serve and protect humanity.

The Petrification Disease

A mysterious and incurable plague begins sweeping through the lower classes of Krat. Victims suffer agonizing transformations, their bodies turning to stone before mutating into aggressive Carcasses.

The Puppet Frenzy

In a single, bloody night, the puppets of Krat mysteriously break the Grand Covenant. They turn on their creators, slaughtering the citizens and leaving the once-great city in ruins.

The Awakening

A solitary blue butterfly guides Sophia's voice to an abandoned train station. A special puppet is awakened, tasked with finding Geppetto and saving what little remains of Krat.
