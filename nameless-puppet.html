Nameless Puppet

Hidden Nightmare

A puppet created for a single purpose, kept secret in the depths. The Nameless Puppet is the ultimate weapon —swift and lethal— representing the true test for the boy who wishes to become human.

Combat Information

  • Location: Under the Abyss
  • Weakness: Electric Blitz
  • Resistance: Fire
  • Drop: Nameless Puppet's Ergo
