Key Figures & Factions

The visionaries, the monsters, and the puppet caught in between.

Key Figures

Protagonist

P (Pinocchio)

Geppetto's masterpiece and the only puppet in Krat not bound by the Grand Covenant. Capable of lying—a uniquely human trait—P must navigate the ruined city, absorbing Ergo and making choices that will determine whether he remains a machine or becomes truly human.

Creator

Geppetto

The legendary inventor who sparked the Puppet Frenzy. He acts as a father figure to P, providing guidance and upgrades. However, his motivations for creating P and his true role in Krat's downfall remain shrouded in dark secrets.

Inventor

Lorenzini Venigni

An eccentric, wealthy industrialist and brilliant engineer. Despite his flamboyant personality, Venigni is deeply traumatized by his past. He becomes a vital ally to P, offering technological support and deciphering the mysteries of the puppet outbreak.

Guide

Sophia

A mysterious, ethereal woman with a deep connection to Ergo. She awakens P at the start of the journey and guides him through Krat. Her powers manipulate time and Ergo, but her true physical state is a tragic secret hidden by the Alchemists.

Companion

Gemini

A cheerful, talkative cricket automaton who serves as P's constant companion and guide. In a city drowning in madness and tragedy, Gemini not only physically lights up Krat's darkest corners but also provides crucial advice and much-needed comic relief.

Antagonist

Simon Manus

The ruthless leader of the Alchemists. He views the Petrification Disease not as a tragedy, but as an opportunity for forced human evolution. Driven by a twisted god complex, he seeks to collect enough Ergo to ascend to godhood.

Factions

Syndicate

The Black Rabbit Brotherhood

A notorious and fiercely loyal gang of Stalkers who rule the Malum District. Characterized by their animal masks and morbid, theatrical combat style, they view themselves as a twisted family. They despise outsiders and become a recurring, chaotic thorn in P's side as he tries to navigate their territory.
